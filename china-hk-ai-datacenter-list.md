## Key Industry Themes

### "East Data, West Compute" (东数西算)
A national strategic initiative directing datacenter construction to western China (Guizhou, Gansu, Inner Mongolia, Ningxia) where land and energy are cheaper, while serving eastern demand centers. This drives massive infrastructure investment by telecoms, cloud providers, and datacenter operators.

### Domestic GPU Substitution
US export controls on advanced AI chips (NVIDIA A100/H100/H200) are accelerating adoption of domestic alternatives — Huawei Ascend, Cambricon MLU, Hygon DCU, Biren, Enflame, Moore Threads. This is reshaping the AI infrastructure supply chain and driving enormous valuation premiums for domestic chip designers.

### Liquid Cooling Adoption
High-density AI GPU racks (30–100+ kW per rack) are driving rapid adoption of liquid cooling, benefiting companies like Envicool, Sugon, Shenling, and Nanjing Canatal. Immersion cooling is emerging for next-generation AI clusters.

### Optical Interconnect Boom
800G and 1.6T optical transceivers are critical for AI cluster interconnect fabrics. Chinese suppliers like Innolight and Eoptolink are global leaders and key beneficiaries of AI datacenter buildout worldwide.

### DeepSeek Effect
DeepSeek's training efficiency breakthroughs (achieving frontier-level performance at a fraction of typical compute cost) have demonstrated that China's AI ecosystem can innovate under hardware constraints, and have intensified demand for domestic AI infrastructure.
