## Key Industry Themes

### "East Data, West Compute" (东数西算)
A national strategic initiative directing datacenter construction to western China (Guizhou, Gansu, Inner Mongolia, Ningxia) where land and energy are cheaper, while serving eastern demand centers. This drives massive infrastructure investment.

### Domestic GPU Substitution
US export controls on advanced AI chips (NVIDIA A100/H100/H200) are accelerating adoption of domestic alternatives (Huawei Ascend, Cambricon MLU, Hygon DCU, Biren, Enflame). This is reshaping the AI infrastructure supply chain.

### Liquid Cooling Adoption
High-density AI GPU racks (30-100+ kW per rack) are driving rapid adoption of liquid cooling, benefiting companies like Envicool, Sugon, Shenling, and Nanjing Canatal.

### Optical Interconnect Boom
800G and 1.6T optical transceivers are critical for AI cluster interconnect fabrics. Chinese suppliers like Innolight are global leaders and key beneficiaries.
