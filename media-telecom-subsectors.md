# Media & Telecom: Sub-Sector Analysis — Addressable Markets & Market Share

*May 2026*

---

## Executive Summary

The combined global media and telecom sector represents over **$6 trillion** in annual revenue, spanning content creation, distribution, advertising, connectivity, and infrastructure. This report maps the key sub-sectors, their total addressable markets (TAMs), growth trajectories, and competitive landscapes as of 2025–2026.

---

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|

**Market Structure by Technology (Global):**

| Technology | Share |
|---|---|
| Cable TV | ~37% |
| Satellite TV | ~30% (projected >50% by 2035 in some markets) |
| IPTV | ~33% (fastest growing) |

**Key Dynamics:**
- Non-pay-TV households surpassed pay-TV households in the U.S. in 2025 for the first time.
- Cord-cutters/cord-nevers: 80.7M U.S. households by end of 2026 vs. 54.3M pay-TV subs.
- U.S. pay TV penetration: ~42% in 2026, down from 88% at peak (2010).
- Streaming revenue ($213B globally) overtook pay TV revenue ($188B) in 2025.
- Generational divide: 64% of adults 65+ subscribe vs. only 16% of adults 18–29.
- Providers pivoting to broadband-first models and integrating streaming apps into set-top boxes.

*Note: Market share ranges vary by report scope (total NI vs. equipment-only). Huawei's share is ~31% of telecom equipment specifically, vs. ~18% of the broader network infrastructure market including services.*

**Sub-Segments:**
- **Radio Access Network (RAN)**: Flat in 2026; 5G core replacing radio as growth driver.
- **Optical Networking**: Growth driven by AI data center connectivity.
- **Core Network (5G SA)**: 20% revenue growth in N. America and EMEA in Q1 2026.
- **Multi-access Edge Computing (MEC)**: Growing; China dominant for implementations.

**Key Dynamics:**
- Geopolitical bifurcation: Western markets (Ericsson/Nokia) vs. China/emerging markets (Huawei/ZTE).
- Hyperscalers entering telco: Alphabet (~$4.2B telco revenue), Amazon (~$3.8B), Microsoft (~$3.0B).
- Open RAN adoption accelerating but still <5% of global deployments.
