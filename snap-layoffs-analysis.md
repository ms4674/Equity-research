# Snap Inc. (SNAP) — Layoffs, Cost Savings & Margin Impact

## Summary

Snap conducted **3 major rounds of layoffs** between August 2022 and February 2024, eliminating roughly **1,700–1,800 positions** in aggregate. The restructuring drove an estimated **~$500M in annualized cost savings** (from the 2022 round alone), incurred cumulative one-time charges of **~$302M**, and contributed to a **~21 pp improvement in operating margin** from the trough in 2023 to 2025.

---

## 1. Rounds of Layoffs

| # | Date | Headcount Cut | % of Workforce | Rationale |
|---|------|--------------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | **Aug 2022** | ~1,200 | 20% | Macro ad-revenue slowdown; strategic reprioritization. Cancelled Snap Originals, Minis, Games, Pixy drone; wound down Zenly & Voisey apps. |
| 2 | **Sep–Nov 2023** | ~160–180 | ~3% | Shut down AR Enterprise division (Sep); small product-team reorg eliminating management layers (Nov). |
| 3 | **Feb 2024** | ~500–530 | 10% | "Reduce hierarchy, promote in-person collaboration"; remote workers disproportionately affected. |

**Total positions eliminated: ~1,860–1,910** (from a peak headcount of ~6,400 in mid-2022 to ~4,800 by mid-2024).

---

## 2. Restructuring Charges (One-Time Costs)

| Period | Charges ($M) | Components |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| 2022 (Round 1) | **$189** | Severance, contract terminations, asset impairments (Q3: $155M, Q4: $34M) |
| 2023 (Round 2) | **$41** | Severance and wind-down costs for AR Enterprise division |
| 2024 (Round 3) | **$72** | Severance and related costs ($55–75M guided; $70M in Q1 2024) |
| **Cumulative Total** | **~$302M** | |

---

## 3. Annualized Cost Savings

### Round 1 (Aug 2022): ~$500M annual run-rate savings
- Snap guided to **$500M in annualized cost savings** relative to Q2 2022 run-rate.
- Savings sourced from headcount reduction, project cancellations (Originals, Games, Pixy, Zenly, Voisey), and operational streamlining.

### Round 2 (2023): ~$25–35M estimated
- Smaller round; AR Enterprise was an early-stage business with limited headcount and operating costs.

### Round 3 (Feb 2024): ~$75–100M estimated
- ~500 employees at an estimated average fully loaded cost of $150–200K/year.
- No official savings figure disclosed; this is a reasonable estimate.

### Total Estimated Annualized Savings: ~$600–635M

## 4. Margin Impact

### Operating Margin (GAAP)

| Year | Revenue ($M) | Operating Loss ($M) | Operating Margin |
|------|-------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| 2021 | 4,117 | (702) | **-17.1%** |
| 2022 | 4,602 | (1,395) | **-30.3%** |
| 2023 | 4,606 | (1,398) | **-30.4%** |
| 2024 | 5,361 | (787) | **-14.7%** |
| 2025 | 5,931 | (532) | **-9.0%** |

- Operating margin improved **~21 pp** from the 2023 trough (-30.4%) to 2025 (-9.0%).
- Restructuring charges inflated 2022 and 2023 losses; excluding them, the underlying trajectory improved sooner.

### Adjusted EBITDA Margin

| Year | Adj. EBITDA ($M) | Adj. EBITDA Margin |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|
| 2022 | 378 | 8.2% |
| 2023 | 162 | 3.5% |
| 2024 | 509 | 9.5% |
| 2025 | 689 | 11.6% |

- Adj. EBITDA margin expanded **~810 bps** from 2023 (3.5%) to 2025 (11.6%), driven by cost discipline post-restructuring combined with revenue re-acceleration.

### Free Cash Flow

| Year | Operating Cash Flow ($M) |
|------|------------------------|
| 2024 | 413 |
| 2025 | 656 |

- Operating cash flow grew 59% YoY in 2025, reflecting the full benefit of the layoff-driven cost structure reset.

## 5. Key Takeaways

1. **Three distinct rounds**: A large 20% RIF in Aug 2022, a smaller 3% cut in late 2023, and a 10% cut in Feb 2024.
2. **~$600M+ in estimated total annualized savings** against ~$302M in one-time restructuring charges — a strong payback ratio (~2x savings/charges).
3. **Margin inflection began in 2024**: GAAP operating margin improved 16 pp YoY (from -30.4% to -14.7%), and Adj. EBITDA margin nearly tripled (3.5% to 9.5%).
4. **Cost savings were amplified by revenue growth**: Revenue grew 16% in 2024 and 11% in 2025, creating positive operating leverage on the leaner cost base.
5. **Not yet profitable on a GAAP basis**: Stock-based compensation (~$1.1B annually) remains a significant drag on GAAP profitability; however, the restructuring has moved the company materially closer to breakeven.
