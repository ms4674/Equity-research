## Search Methodology

This analysis was conducted using:
1. **SEC EDGAR Full-Text Search (EFTS)** — Queried for filings containing terms related to cloud market share, GPU allocation, hyperscaler comparison, and neocloud positioning
2. **Company Investor Relations** — Reviewed quarterly earnings presentations and annual reports from NVIDIA, CoreWeave, Oracle, AMD, and infrastructure companies
3. **Web Search** — Identified third-party research reports (Synergy Research, SemiAnalysis, ARPU Intelligence, Morgan Stanley) that provide the underlying data used in public filings
4. **Thematic Matching** — Identified presentations sharing the reference slide's core themes: (a) cloud revenue market share comparison, (b) GPU allocation by customer/provider, (c) emergence of new hyperscalers/neoclouds
