# Snowflake-Palantir Customer Base Overlap & AI Revenue Contribution Analysis

**Companies Covered:** Snowflake (SNOW), Palantir (PLTR), Databricks (private)
**Date:** March 31, 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Snowflake and Palantir formalized a strategic partnership in October 2025, acknowledging meaningful customer overlap and complementary capabilities across their enterprise customer bases. While granular overlap data is not publicly disclosed, the partnership signals that the two companies share a substantial set of enterprise customers — particularly in the Fortune 500 and public sector. AI products remain at different maturity stages across all three companies: Snowflake's AI revenue is nascent (~2.3% of revenue), Palantir's AIP is the primary growth engine driving 137% U.S. commercial revenue growth, and Databricks' AI products have reached ~$1.4B annualized run-rate (~26% of total revenue run-rate).

## 4. Comparative AI Revenue Contribution Summary

| Metric | Snowflake | Palantir | Databricks |
|--------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Total revenue (latest annual)** | $4.68B (FY2026) | $4.475B (FY2025) | ~$5.4B ARR (Feb 2026) |
| **Revenue growth rate** | 29% YoY | 56% YoY | 65% YoY (Q4) |
| **AI revenue contribution** | ~2.3% (~$100M ARR) | Not disclosed (dominant growth driver) | ~26% (~$1.4B ARR) |
| **AI as growth catalyst** | Emerging | Primary | Major |
| **AI customer adoption** | 9,100 accounts | 954 total customers (AIP-driven) | 40%+ on agentic AI |
| **AI product maturity** | Early (GA products shipping) | Mature (AIP + Bootcamps proven) | Mature (production AI at scale) |
| **Forward AI revenue trajectory** | Accelerating from low base | Embedded in all growth | Rapidly scaling |

### Key Takeaways

1. **Databricks leads in quantified AI revenue contribution** at ~26% of total revenue, with the clearest product-revenue attribution and the fastest absolute AI revenue growth trajectory ($1.0B to $1.4B in ~5 months).

2. **Palantir's AI contribution is the most strategically embedded** — while not separately disclosed, AIP is the singular driver behind the company's commercial acceleration. Effectively 100% of Palantir's go-to-market is now AI-led, making the entire company an "AI revenue story" even if the accounting doesn't isolate it.

3. **Snowflake's AI contribution is earliest-stage** at ~2.3% of revenue, but the company has the largest customer base (12,600+) and broadest enterprise penetration, providing a massive surface area for AI product upsell. The 9,100 accounts already using AI features represent strong early adoption signals.

4. **The Snowflake-Palantir partnership creates a powerful combined offering** — Snowflake's data scale + Palantir's decision-intelligence layer — that directly competes with Databricks' unified data + AI platform approach. The overlap in their customer bases was a key motivator for the partnership.

## 5. Strategic Implications

### For Investors
- Palantir commands the highest AI premium in its valuation due to AIP's demonstrated impact on growth acceleration
- Databricks (pre-IPO) offers the most balanced AI revenue story with clear quantification
- Snowflake's AI upside is largely future-facing — the 9,100-account AI adoption base represents significant unrealized revenue potential

### For Enterprise Customers
- Customers using both Snowflake and Palantir can now leverage integrated workflows, reducing data movement and operational friction
- The Snowflake-Palantir-Databricks competitive dynamic benefits customers through aggressive AI feature development and pricing competition
- All three companies are converging on agentic AI as the next major capability frontier

### Competitive Dynamics
- Snowflake + Palantir partnership is a strategic response to Databricks' unified data + AI positioning
- Databricks' $100B+ valuation and IPO trajectory add urgency to the competitive landscape
- The cloud data + AI market is projected to reach $183B by 2035 at 20.71% CAGR, leaving substantial room for all three companies to grow
