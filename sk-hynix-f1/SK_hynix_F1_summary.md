# SK hynix Inc. — Form F-1 (U.S. IPO / Nasdaq ADS Listing): Key Takeaways

> **Note on the filing.** SK hynix is already a public company listed on the Korea Exchange
> (KOSPI: 000660). The document commonly referred to as its "S-1" is in fact a **Form F-1**
> registration statement (the foreign-private-issuer equivalent of an S-1), filed with the
> U.S. SEC on **June 24, 2026** to list **American Depositary Shares (ADSs)** on the
> **Nasdaq Global Select Market** under the ticker **"SKHY."** This document summarizes that
> filing. Companion file: [`SK_hynix_F1_key_tables.xlsx`](./SK_hynix_F1_key_tables.xlsx).
>
> Currency: figures are in Korean Won (W) unless noted. The prospectus translates Won to USD
> at **W1,523.5 = US$1.00** (NY Fed noon buying rate on March 31, 2026). Financials are IFRS,
> consolidated. Many deal-specific numbers (final price, share count, net proceeds) are blank
> in the F-1 and are set at pricing/bookbuilding.

---

## 1. What the deal is

- **Issuer / security:** SK hynix Inc. is offering common shares represented by **ADSs**, with
  **each ADS representing 1/10 of one common share** (10 ADSs = 1 share).
- **Size:** Board resolved a **maximum of 17,790,000 new common shares (~2.50%)** of the
  712,702,365 shares outstanding. The cap exists because controlling shareholder **SK square**
  must keep ≥20% ownership under Korea's Monopoly Regulation and Fair Trade Act.
- **Listings:** Nasdaq ticker **"SKHY"**; common shares remain on KOSPI (**000660**) and
  Luxembourg GDRs trade as **"HYNSE."**
- **Underwriters:** BofA Securities, Citigroup, Goldman Sachs (Asia), J.P. Morgan.
  **Depositary:** Citibank, N.A. **Lock-up:** 90 days.
- **Reported intent (Korean regulatory filing / press, June 24, 2026):** raise up to
  **~W45.45 trillion (≈ US$29.4 billion)**, ADRs priced around **255,500 won**, targeting a
  **Nasdaq debut on July 10, 2026** — potentially the largest ADR offering on record. Final
  size/price subject to bookbuilding.
- **Use of proceeds:** fund **W45.5 trillion** of Korean production-facility capex (Fab 1 at
  the Yongin complex; the P&T7 advanced-packaging plant in Cheongju) plus **~W11.9 trillion**
  of **EUV scanner** purchases (delivery by Dec 2027). Shortfalls funded by operating cash
  flow and debt.

## 2. Why it matters — the AI memory story

- SK hynix is **one of the world's two largest memory makers** and the **clear HBM leader**.
  Q1 2026 market shares (IDC): **DRAM incl. HBM #2 at 29.1%**, **HBM #1 at 56.4%**,
  **NAND #2 at 18.5%.**
- It was **first to mass-produce HBM3 and HBM3E**, **developed HBM4 in 2025**, and is
  investing in **HBM4E / custom HBM** with base-die integration for AI accelerator customers
  (e.g., relationships across the GPU/AI ecosystem).
- DRAM is **~77% of revenue**; the mix has shifted hard toward high-ASP HBM and server DRAM,
  driving margin expansion.

**Takeaways:**
- A dramatic **cyclical recovery**: from a **W9.1 trillion net loss in 2023** to **record
  FY2025** revenue of **W97.1tn** (+46.8% YoY) and operating profit of **W47.2tn** (~doubled).
- **Q1 2026 was extraordinary** — revenue **+198% YoY**, gross margin **79.3%**, net profit
  **W40.3tn** (which already exceeds full-year FY2025 net profit, partly due to very large
  **finance/FX income of W17.1tn** in the quarter).
- **Balance sheet de-levered and cash-rich:** total assets **W222.8tn** and total equity
  **W164.4tn** at Mar 31, 2026; cash & equivalents **W21.2tn**; total borrowings fell while
  retained earnings climbed to **W148.7tn**.
- **Heavy reinvestment:** investing outflows surged (FY2025 net investing outflow **W48.1tn**),
  reflecting the capacity buildout that this offering helps fund.

## 4. Revenue mix

- **By product (FY2025):** DRAM **77.1%** / NAND **21.3%** / Other **1.6%**. DRAM's share rose
  from 63.4% (2023) → 67.6% (2024) → 77.1% (2025) as HBM scaled.
- **By region (FY2025, by location of sales entity):** United States **68.8%**, China **19.7%**,
  Asia ex-China/Korea **7.4%**, Europe **2.0%**, Korea **2.0%**. U.S. exposure has risen sharply
  (47.0% in 2023 → 68.8% in 2025) with AI data-center demand.
- **Customer concentration:** in Q1 2026 the two largest customers were **14.8%** and **12.4%**
  of revenue; in FY2025 the single largest customer was **23.9%**.

## 5. Ownership & governance

- **SK square owns 20.50%** (part of SK Group, ultimately controlled by Chey Tae-won); other
  notable holders: National Pension Service 8.06%, BlackRock 5.11%, Capital Research 3.53%.
- As a **foreign private issuer**, SK hynix is exempt from certain U.S. corporate-governance
  rules. Related-party transactions are scrutinized by the Korea Fair Trade Commission.
- **Shareholder return policy (FY2025–2027):** annual dividend of **W1,500/share**, paid as
  four quarterly installments of **W375**, with potential for additional returns on excess cash.

## 6. Key risks (from the F-1)

- **Industry cyclicality / oversupply** and ASP volatility (the 2023 loss illustrates the
  downside of the cycle).
- **Concentration on AI capex:** a slowdown in hyperscaler AI infrastructure spending, or
  technology shifts that reduce memory intensity, would hit demand for HBM/server DRAM.
- **Customer & geographic concentration** in the U.S. and China; tariffs, anti-dumping and
  export-control / geopolitical risk (incl. U.S.–China tensions, China fabs in Wuxi/Dalian).
- **Capacity-expansion execution & capital intensity** (Yongin, Cheongju P&T7, Indiana packaging).
- **FX risk** (Won/USD; large unhedged exposures swing reported finance income/expense).
- **Korea-specific risks** (economy, North Korea tensions) and **ADS-specific risks** (deposit
  agreement amendments, re-deposit limits, FPI governance carve-outs).

## 7. Production footprint

- **Fabs:** Icheon & Cheongju (Korea), Wuxi & Dalian (China); back-end assembly/test in Icheon,
  Cheongju and Chongqing (China).
- **Expansion:** M15X (Cheongju) cleanroom opened Oct 2025, wafer input began Q1 2026; first
  **Yongin** fab cleanroom expected Q1 2027; **P&T7** packaging (Cheongju) by end-2027;
  **Indiana, U.S.** advanced-packaging plant operations from 2H 2028.
- **People:** **47,639** consolidated full-time employees (35,321 Korea; 11,333 China; 591 U.S.).
