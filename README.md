# Equity-research

## TSMC Fab Operational Costs: Phoenix, AZ vs. Taipei/Hsinchu, Taiwan

Comprehensive comparison of TSMC semiconductor fabrication operational costs between the Phoenix, Arizona (Fab 21) and Taipei/Hsinchu, Taiwan facilities.

### Files

- **`TSMC_Phoenix_vs_Taiwan_Fab_Costs.xlsx`** — Full Excel spreadsheet with three tabs:
  - **Cost Comparison** — Detailed side-by-side comparison across all cost categories
  - **Sources & References** — 42 cited sources with URLs and dates
  - **Key Findings** — Top 10 summary findings
- **`TSMC_Phoenix_vs_Taiwan_Fab_Costs.csv`** — CSV version of the cost comparison for quick viewing

### Cost Categories Covered

| Category | Key Finding |
|---|---|
| Electricity | AZ ~42% cheaper ($0.079 vs $0.136/kWh) |
| Water | AZ ~5-6x more expensive (~$2.18 vs ~$0.37/m³) |
| Natural Gas | AZ ~46% cheaper |
| Labor | AZ ~64% higher (engineers), but <2% of wafer cost |
| Equipment & Depreciation | Same globally (>67% of wafer cost) |
| Materials & Chemicals | AZ ~10-15% higher |
| Taxes & Incentives | Both generous; AZ has $6.6B direct CHIPS Act funding |
| Construction (CapEx) | AZ 4-5x higher |
| **Overall Operating Cost** | **AZ ~10% higher (per TechInsights Mar 2025)** |

### Data Sources

All data sourced from public reports (2024–2025) including TechInsights, EIA, City of Phoenix, Taipei Times, TrendForce, Reuters, Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, Tax Foundation, Deloitte, and others. See the Sources tab in the spreadsheet for complete citations.
