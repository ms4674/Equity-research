# Equity Research

## Agentic Commerce Protocol and Its Impact on Performance Ads and Visual Search

An in-depth analysis of how agentic commerce protocols (UCP, ACP) are reshaping digital advertising economics, disrupting retail media networks, and transforming visual search -- with investment implications for Google, Meta, Amazon, Shopify, and Stripe.

### Contents

- [Full Analysis](agentic-commerce-protocol-impact.md)

### Key Topics Covered

1. **Protocol Landscape** -- UCP (Google/Shopify) vs. ACP (OpenAI/Stripe) adoption, capabilities, and strategic positioning
2. **Performance Advertising Disruption** -- Shift from CPC/CPM to protocol-layer monetization; semantic governance constraints
3. **Retail Media Network Transformation** -- From visual sponsored placements to structured data sponsorship
4. **Visual Search Bifurcation** -- Human-facing visual search (growing) vs. agent-mediated visual perception (emerging)
5. **Platform Strategic Responses** -- Capital allocation and strategy for Google ($175-185B), Meta ($115-135B), and Amazon
6. **Revenue Model Migration** -- From advertising-funded to transaction-funded commerce (5-10% facilitation fees)
7. **Investment Implications** -- Structural winners, risks, and transitional dynamics through 2030+
