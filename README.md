# Data center GW capacity: United States vs international

Live, under-construction, and planned data-center capacity, in gigawatts of IT load, split between the United States and the rest of the world. Each country has a political-risk score and a HIGH-risk flag.

The workbook is `datacenter_gw_capacity_us_vs_international.xlsx`. Rebuild it with:

```bash
python3 build_datacenter_capacity_workbook.py
```

Figures are Cushman & Wakefield's H1 2026 regional updates, with China also shown on a full-fleet basis (SemiAnalysis, Rystad). Political-risk evidence runs through 7 November 2026. Sources, the scoring rubric, and the caveats are inside the workbook.

Under construction is the near-committed book: 91.7% of the Americas book, and nearly all of the Asia-Pacific book, was pre-leased before delivery. The planned pipeline is announced capacity, not a commitment.
