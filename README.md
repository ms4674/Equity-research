# Equity Research

## Reports

- [Power Procurement Strategy for Data Centers: Hyperscalers and Neoclouds](power-procurement-strategy-data-centers.md) — Analysis of power procurement models, competitive positioning, and investment implications across hyperscalers (Microsoft, AWS, Google, Meta) and neoclouds (CoreWeave, Crusoe, Lambda) as power replaces compute as the primary constraint on AI infrastructure expansion. (May 2026)

- [Power-to-Tokens and Revenue Analysis: 1 GW Economics](power-to-tokens-and-revenue-analysis.md) — Quantitative analysis of the conversion from 1 GW of data center power to token generation throughput (~258 trillion tokens/year on B200) and GPU cloud rental revenue ($8-12B/year full-stack). Includes GPU count calculations, tokens-per-watt efficiency metrics, revenue sensitivity by business model, and the value multiplication chain from electron to API dollar. (May 2026)

- [AI vs. Traditional Data Center Economics: Rack Density and OpEx](ai-vs-traditional-datacenter-economics.md) — Comparative analysis of AI data centers (80-132 kW/rack) vs. traditional cloud (8-15 kW/rack). Covers the 10-15x density gap, cooling architecture evolution, component-by-component OpEx breakdown (AI runs 1.5-2x higher facility OpEx/MW but generates 4-6x revenue/MW), CapEx comparison, and implications for data center REITs and operators. (May 2026)

- [Key Drivers of PUE for AI Data Centers](pue-drivers-ai-datacenters.md) — Deep-dive into the five key PUE drivers (cooling architecture, power distribution, IT equipment, climate/site, operational optimization), with component-by-component PUE breakdown, sensitivity analysis, financial case for PUE improvements, and how hyperscalers achieve 1.04-1.10. Key insight: liquid cooling is mandatory for AI density and inherently more efficient, structurally linking the density constraint to better PUE. (May 2026)
