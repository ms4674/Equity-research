# Equity-research

## GPU/TPU Cloud Market Share Analysis

`gpu_tpu_cloud_market_share.xlsx` compares GPU/TPU allocations and cloud infrastructure market share across hyperscalers (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Oracle) and neoclouds (CoreWeave, Nebius, Nscale, Lambda, Crusoe, Iren).

### Spreadsheet Contents

| Sheet | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **GPU-TPU Allocation** | Detailed GPU fleet sizes (H100-equivalent), deployed models, power capacity, CapEx, and custom silicon for each provider |
| **Cloud Market Share** | Cloud IaaS market share %, revenue, growth rates, and GPU cloud share for all providers |
| **GPU vs Cloud Share** | Side-by-side comparison of GPU fleet size against cloud market share with observations |
| **2026 CapEx Summary** | Capital expenditure guidance and AI infrastructure investment targets |
| **Sources & Notes** | Data sources, methodology, and estimation notes |

### Key Findings

- **Google** has the largest GPU/TPU fleet (3.5-4.2M H100-equiv) driven by TPU deployments, but only 12% cloud market share — most capacity is consumed internally.
- **Amazon** leads cloud market share (31%) but has a comparatively smaller GPU fleet (1.3-1.6M), diversifying with Trainium custom silicon.
- **Microsoft** is augmenting its 2.5-3.1M GPU fleet with $60B+ in neocloud partnerships to close a capacity gap that extends into mid-2026.
- **CoreWeave** (250K+ GPUs) is the largest neocloud, growing revenue ~420% YoY with <1% overall cloud share but ~2% of GPU cloud share.
- Combined hyperscaler AI CapEx for 2026 is projected at $660-690B, with ~75% directed at GPU/AI infrastructure.

Data as of Q1 2026.
