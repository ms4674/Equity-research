# AI Networking Market Size & Share of Hyperscale Capex

## Executive Summary

The AI networking market (switches, optics, NICs for AI clusters) has grown from roughly **$6B in 2023 to ~$20B in 2025**, and is projected to reach **$28-30B by 2026**. As a share of total hyperscale capex, AI networking represents approximately **3-5%** of total spend, or roughly **7-11% of AI-specific infrastructure capex**. Despite being a relatively small slice of overall capex, it is one of the fastest-growing segments, with Ethernet AI networking revenue surging 150%+ year-over-year in 2024.

| Year | InfiniBand Share (AI Back-end) | Ethernet Share (AI Back-end) |

## 5. Key Themes & Investment Implications

### Networking is Small but Critical
At 3-5% of total hyperscale capex (or ~11% of AI-specific infrastructure spend), networking is a relatively small line item—dwarfed by GPU spend (~40%) and data center construction (~27%). However, it is mission-critical: GPU utilization in large clusters drops significantly with suboptimal network fabrics.

### Ethernet is Winning
The shift from InfiniBand to Ethernet for AI back-end networking is the defining trend. Ethernet's share of AI back-end switches went from ~20% in 2022 to >67% in 2025. This benefits Arista, Broadcom (switching ASICs), and Celestica, while potentially pressuring NVIDIA's networking margins over time.

### Speed Transitions Are Accelerating
- **800G** dominated 2025 shipments and reached 20M ports in 3 years (vs. 6-7 years for 400G)
- **1.6T** expected to begin shipping H2 2026
- Each speed transition creates an upgrade cycle that drives networking spend

### Growth Decoupling
AI networking growth (~40-65% YoY) is outpacing total hyperscale capex growth (~50-73% YoY) in certain years, but the ratio has stabilized around 4-5%. This suggests networking spend scales roughly proportionally with overall infrastructure investment.

### Networking Spend Could Inflect Higher
Several factors could push networking above the 5% threshold:
- Ultra-large clusters (100K+ GPU) require disproportionately more networking
- Scale-up networking (intra-node interconnects) is a rapidly growing new category
- 1.6T optics transitions will raise ASPs
- Custom silicon (e.g., Google TPU pods, Amazon Trainium) may require more networking relative to compute
