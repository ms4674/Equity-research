# Meta Platforms (META) — Layoffs: Rounds, Percentages & Margin Impact

## Summary

Meta has conducted **5 rounds of layoffs** since November 2022, cutting approximately **25,000+ positions** from a peak headcount of ~87,000. The cumulative workforce reduction is roughly **29% of peak headcount**. Restructuring charges totaled ~$3.5B in 2023 alone, but the "Year of Efficiency" drove operating margin from 25% (2022) to 42% (2024) — a **~17 pp expansion** — making it one of the most successful cost restructurings in large-cap tech history.

---

## 1. Layoff Rounds — Percentage Breakdown

| # | Date | Employees Cut | % of Workforce | Pre-Layoff Headcount | Rationale |
|---|------|--------------|----------------|---------------------|-----------|
| 1 | **Nov 2022** | ~11,000 | **13%** | ~87,000 | Post-pandemic ad downturn; over-hiring during COVID boom. First layoffs in Meta's 18-year history. |
| 2 | **Mar 2023** | ~10,000 | **~13%** | ~76,000 | "Year of Efficiency" Phase 2; flattened management layers, cancelled 5,000 open roles, killed low-priority projects. |
| 3 | **Jan 2025** | ~3,600 | **~5%** | ~72,000 | Performance-based cuts targeting "lowest performers"; planned to backfill throughout 2025. |
| 4 | **Jan 2026** | ~1,500 | **~10% of Reality Labs** | ~79,000 (total); ~15,000 (Reality Labs) | Closed 3 VR game studios (Twisted Pixel, Sanzaru, Armature). Shift from metaverse to AI. |
| 5 | **Mar 2026** | ~700 | **<1%** | ~77,000 | Targeted cuts across Reality Labs, Facebook, recruiting, sales, global operations. |

### Cumulative Total

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total positions cut (2022–2026) | **~25,000–26,800** |
| Peak headcount (Q3 2022) | ~87,000 |
| Trough headcount (end 2023) | ~67,317 |
| Current headcount (end 2025) | ~78,865 |
| **Cumulative cut as % of peak** | **~29–31%** |

### Pending: Rumored 20% Further Cut (2026)

In March 2026, Reuters reported Meta was planning an additional **~20% workforce reduction (~15,000–15,800 positions)** to fund $600B in AI data center buildout through 2028. Meta spokesperson called it "speculative reporting about theoretical approaches." Not yet confirmed but stock rose ~3% on the report.

## 2. Headcount Trajectory

| Year-End | Employees | YoY Change |
|----------|----------|------------|
| 2019 | 44,942 | — |
| 2020 | 58,604 | +30% |
| 2021 | 71,970 | +23% |
| 2022 | 86,482 | +20% |
| 2023 | 67,317 | **-22%** |
| 2024 | 74,067 | +10% |
| 2025 | 78,865 | +6% |

Meta nearly doubled headcount from 2019 to 2022, then cut 22% in 2023 before gradually re-hiring — but into AI/engineering roles, not the management and support functions that were cut.

## 3. Restructuring Charges

|--------|---------|

| FY 2023 | **$3.45B** (severance, facilities, other) |

The $3.45B in 2023 restructuring charges were a significant one-time cost but were quickly earned back through operating expense discipline.

## 4. Margin Impact

### Operating Margin (GAAP)

| Year | Revenue ($B) | Operating Income ($B) | Operating Margin | YoY Change |
|------|-------------|----------------------|-----------------|------------|
| 2021 | 117.9 | 46.8 | **39.7%** | — |
| 2022 | 116.6 | 28.9 | **24.8%** | -14.9 pp |
| 2023 | 134.9 | 46.8 | **34.7%** | +9.9 pp |
| 2024 | 164.5 | 69.4 | **42.2%** | +7.5 pp |
| 2025 | 201.0 | 83.3 | **41.4%** | -0.8 pp |

- **2022 was the trough** at 24.8% — driven by metaverse spending, ad market weakness, and the beginning of restructuring costs.
- **2023 saw a 10 pp recovery** as the Year of Efficiency took hold: headcount fell 22%, but revenue grew 16%.
- **2024 reached a record 42.2%** — a full **17.4 pp improvement** from the 2022 low, even as Meta resumed hiring selectively.
- **2025 held steady at 41.4%** despite 24% growth in total expenses, as revenue grew 22% to $201B.

### Key Efficiency Metrics

| Metric | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|--------|------|------|------|------|
| Revenue per employee | $1.35M | $2.00M | $2.22M | $2.55M |
| Operating income per employee | $335K | $695K | $937K | $1.06M |

Revenue per employee nearly **doubled** from 2022 to 2025 — the clearest evidence that the layoffs structurally improved Meta's operating leverage.

---

## 5. Key Takeaways

1. **~29–31% of peak workforce eliminated** across 5 rounds since Nov 2022, with ~21,000 cut in the first two rounds alone (Nov 2022 + Mar 2023).
2. **Operating margin expanded ~17 pp** from the 2022 trough (24.8%) to the 2024 peak (42.2%), making the "Year of Efficiency" arguably the most impactful cost restructuring in big tech.
3. **Revenue per employee nearly doubled** ($1.35M → $2.55M), showing that cuts were concentrated in non-revenue-generating roles.
4. **$3.45B in one-time restructuring charges** paid for themselves many times over — operating income increased by $40B+ annually from 2022 to 2024.
5. **Headcount is re-growing**, but selectively: from 67,317 (end 2023) to 78,865 (end 2025), with new hires concentrated in AI/ML engineering. Meta is now a structurally leaner company doing more with fewer people.
6. **Another ~20% cut may be coming** in 2026 to fund $600B in AI capex, which would bring total reductions to ~40%+ of the 2022 peak if confirmed.
